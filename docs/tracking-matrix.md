# Tracking Matrix

Marketing must complete this matrix before publishing tags in Google Tag Manager.

| Tag name | Vendor | Purpose | Consent category | Trigger | Data collected | Remarketing/audiences? | Conversion event? | Privacy/cookie disclosure needed | Owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example: GA4 page view | Google Analytics | Site analytics | Analytics | Page view after consent | Page URL, referrer, device/browser data | No | No | Google Analytics analytics disclosure | Marketing | Draft |

## Rules

- Do not publish a tag without a consent category.
- Do not publish a tag without GTM consent checks.
- Do not send health conditions, appointment details, free-text form data, names, phone numbers, email addresses, or patient-identifying data.
- Keep this matrix aligned with the `/privacidade-e-cookies` page.
- Treat GTM publish access as privileged script access.
